What Numica is

Numica is a maths practice app for children in Grades 1 to 10, built around Ontario's maths curriculum. A parent creates the family account and adds each child; the child practises maths under it.

Deleting your account and your children's data

You can permanently delete your family's account and everything under it — every child's name and grade, all practice records, and any feedback you sent — yourself, inside the app, at any time. There is no need to email us first, and no grace period once you confirm.

  1. Open Numica, go to your child's Profile tab, and tap Grown-ups › at the bottom.
  2. Enter your parent PIN.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account and all data.
  5. Confirm Delete everything.

This is behind your parent PIN on purpose, so a child cannot delete the family's account by accident. If you have lost your PIN, the same Settings screen offers a way back in through your email.
